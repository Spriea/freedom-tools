Privacy Policy

本政策说明 Aquarium Log 如何处理你的鱼缸记录、照片、提醒和购买信息。

最后更新：2026 年 8 月 14 日 · 适用产品：Aquarium Log

我们的原则

Aquarium Log 以本地优先方式运行：你的养缸记录保存在设备上，不需要创建账号。

我们处理的信息

你创建的鱼缸资料、水质记录、养护日志、提醒、照片和显示偏好用于在你的设备上提供应用功能。除非你主动使用“分享 / 导出 / 备份恢复”等系统操作，应用不会将这些内容上传到我们的服务器。

照片、相机与相册

当你主动拍摄、选择或保存图片时，应用才会请求相机或照片访问权限。导入的图片会复制到应用管理的本地媒体目录，用于鱼缸封面和日志；不会删除你的系统相册原图。

通知

当你为提醒启用通知时，提醒会在设备本地安排。通知内容用于显示相应鱼缸、任务和到期信息。你可以随时在系统设置中关闭通知权限。

内购

Lifetime Pro 购买和恢复购买由 Apple StoreKit 处理。我们不会接收或存储你的支付卡信息；购买状态以 Apple 的交易状态为准。

备份与导出

备份、CSV 和 PDF 报告均由你主动发起，并通过系统分享或文件界面保存。备份不包含购买状态或 StoreKit 交易信息。

联系我们

如有隐私相关问题，请通过 somerking@foxmail.com 联系我们。

Our principle

Aquarium Log is local-first: your aquarium records stay on your device and no account is required.

Information we process

Aquarium profiles, water records, care logs, reminders, photos, and display preferences are used on your device to provide app features. We do not upload this content to our servers unless you actively use a system sharing, export, backup, or restore action.

Camera and photo library

The app requests camera or photo-library access only when you choose to take, select, or save a photo. Imported images are copied into app-managed local storage for covers and journal entries; your original library photos are not deleted.

Notifications

When you enable a reminder, notifications are scheduled locally on your device. Their content identifies the relevant aquarium, task, and due information. You can disable notifications at any time in system settings.

In-app purchase

Lifetime Pro purchases and restores are processed by Apple StoreKit. We do not receive or store payment-card information; Apple transaction state is the source of truth for purchase status.

Backup and export

Backups, CSV files, and PDF reports are created only when you request them and are saved or shared through system interfaces. Backups do not include purchase status or StoreKit transaction data.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact somerking@foxmail.com.

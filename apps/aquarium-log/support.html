常见问题

如何恢复 Lifetime Pro？

打开“设置”→“Aquarium Log Pro”，然后选择“恢复购买”。恢复完成后，权益会自动更新。

提醒没有出现怎么办？

请在系统设置中允许 Aquarium Log 发送通知，并确认对应提醒已开启、时间与重复周期正确。

如何备份或导出数据？

打开“设置”→“数据与导出”。备份与恢复、CSV 导出和 PDF 报告属于 Aquarium Log Pro 功能。

照片无法添加或保存？

当应用请求相机或照片权限时请选择允许；如果此前拒绝过，请前往系统设置重新开启相应权限。

Frequently asked questions

How do I restore Lifetime Pro?

Open Settings → Aquarium Log Pro, then choose Restore Purchases. Your benefits update automatically after a successful restore.

Why are my reminders not appearing?

Allow Aquarium Log notifications in system settings, then confirm that the reminder is enabled and its time and recurrence are correct.

How do I back up or export my data?

Open Settings → Data & Export. Backup & Restore, CSV export, and PDF reports are Aquarium Log Pro features.

Why can’t I add or save a photo?

Allow camera or photo-library access when prompted. If access was denied previously, re-enable the relevant permission in system settings.

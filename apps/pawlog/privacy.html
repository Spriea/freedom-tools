隐私政策

Privacy policy

更新日期 / Updated: 2026-10-22

本地资料Local data

宠物档案、头像、八类实际记录、用药疗程、提醒及完成状态主要保存在 App 私有存储中。PawLog 不要求注册账号，也不将这些资料自动上传到开发者服务器。删除宠物会删除其关联记录及不再被引用的头像。卸载 App 可能移除本地资料，请先备份。

Pet profiles, avatars, the eight supported record types, medication courses, reminders and completion states are primarily held in private app storage. PawLog does not require an account or automatically upload these details to developer servers. Deleting a pet removes its related records and unreferenced avatar. Uninstalling the app may remove local data; back it up first.

权限与通知Permissions and notifications

仅在你主动拍照、选择头像或启用提醒时使用相关权限。通知标题可能出现在锁屏；可在系统设置管理权限与预览。拒绝相机、相册或通知权限不影响无头像档案及 App 内提醒计划。

Camera, photo selection and notification access are used for actions you initiate. Reminder titles may appear on the lock screen; manage permission and previews in system settings. Declining these permissions does not prevent profiles without avatars or in-app reminder plans.

备份、分享与文件Backups, sharing and files

选择文件恢复、创建 ZIP 和系统分享均由你主动发起。ZIP 未加密，分享给第三方后将受接收方的处理方式影响。恢复采用完整替换，确认前会校验并展示内容预览。没有自动备份或云同步。系统自身的设备备份受你的系统设置和平台政策控制。

File import, ZIP creation and system sharing are initiated by you. ZIP files are not encrypted; third parties you share with handle them under their own practices. Restore replaces current journal data after validation, preview and confirmation. PawLog provides no automatic backup or cloud sync. Operating-system device backups are governed by your platform settings and policies.

商店与外部服务Stores and external services

购买与恢复购买通过平台商店处理。PawLog 保存独立权益缓存，不在 ZIP 中包含购买凭据或 Pro 缓存。检查更新会在商店信息配置可用时查询平台服务。主动打开网站、发送支持邮件或使用系统分享后，对应平台或服务可能接收必要信息。PawLog 未接入广告或行为分析 SDK。

Purchases and purchase restoration are handled by platform stores. PawLog keeps a separate access cache and excludes purchase credentials and Pro cache from ZIP backups. Update checks query the platform when valid store configuration is available. Opening websites, emailing support or using system sharing may send necessary information to the selected platform or service. PawLog does not integrate advertising or behavioral analytics SDKs.

支持与隐私请求Support and privacy requests

发送邮件时，邮箱服务会处理你主动提供的邮件地址和内容。请勿发送不必要的个人信息、医疗资料或完整备份。你可以在 App 中管理和删除本地资料。如需咨询，请联系 somerking@foxmail.com。

When you email support, email services process the address and contents you provide. Avoid sending unnecessary personal information, medical details or full backups. Manage and delete local data in the app. For questions, contact somerking@foxmail.com.

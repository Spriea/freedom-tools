常见问题

如何恢复 Pro？

打开“设置”→“装修防超支 Pro”，选择“恢复购买”，并确认设备登录的是购买时使用的应用商店账号。

付款提醒没有出现怎么办？

请确认付款计划仍为待处理状态、提醒时间尚未过去，并在系统设置中允许 App 发送通知。

如何备份或导出项目？

打开“设置”中的数据管理入口，可使用备份与恢复、CSV、PDF 或分享图。生成的文件需由你通过系统界面选择保存位置或接收应用。

为什么无法添加资料图片？

请在系统设置中允许相机或照片权限，并确认设备仍有足够存储空间。

不同币种会自动换算吗？

不会。每个项目使用所选币种展示和保存原始金额，App 不获取汇率，也不进行货币换算。

Frequently asked questions

How do I restore Pro?

Open Settings → Renovation Budget Guard Pro, choose Restore Purchases, and make sure the device uses the same store account that made the purchase.

Why did a payment reminder not appear?

Confirm that the payment plan is still pending, its reminder time has not passed, and notifications are allowed in system settings.

How do I back up or export a project?

Open Data Management in Settings to use Backup & Restore, CSV, PDF, or a share image. Use the system interface to choose where generated files are saved or sent.

Why can’t I add an attachment image?

Allow camera or photo access in system settings and make sure the device has enough free storage.

Does the app convert currencies?

No. Each project displays and stores raw amounts in its selected currency. The app does not retrieve exchange rates or convert amounts.

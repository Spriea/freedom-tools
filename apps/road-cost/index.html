ZIP 备份包含行程资料、费用和关联费用图片，不包含完整 GPS 轨迹点；当前没有自动 iCloud 备份。

ZIP backups contain trip details, expenses, and associated expense images, but not complete GPS track points. Automatic iCloud backup is not provided.

iOS 使用 Apple 地图。后台记录需要相应权限，GPS 信号和系统设置可能影响连续性与精度；持续定位会增加耗电。

iOS maps use Apple MapKit. Background recording requires permission. GPS reception and system settings affect continuity and accuracy; continuous location use increases battery consumption.

界面支持 English 和简体中文。所有语言的费用均使用人民币（CNY），不自动换算货币。

Available in English and Simplified Chinese. Expense amounts use CNY in both languages, without automatic currency conversion.

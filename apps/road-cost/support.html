常见问题

为什么需要定位权限？

用于记录 GPS 路线和计算里程。若希望锁屏或后台继续记录，需要允许相应的后台定位权限。

为什么重开 App 后显示“轨迹记录已停止”？

如果 App 被系统终止、手动关闭或定位中断，行程本身仍会保留。重新打开后可以恢复轨迹记录，已保存里程不会自动清零。

为什么隧道中的里程可能少一些？

隧道、地下车库等环境可能暂时没有 GPS。为了避免巨大跳点和虚假里程，随行迹可能忽略长时间定位中断期间的跨段距离。

为什么停车时可能有少量里程变化？

GPS 静止时也可能漂移。App 会过滤短距离和低质量定位，但复杂环境下仍可能有少量误差。

结束行程后还会继续定位吗？

结束行程后会停止本次持续轨迹记录。系统可能仍展示近期定位使用记录，但 App 不应继续为已结束行程写入轨迹。

如何查看已记录路线？

在行程列表打开行程，查看已保存的路线、里程、时间及费用。当前版本不展示地图更多工具入口。

地图位置为什么会有偏差？

iOS 使用 Apple 地图。GPS 信号、建筑遮挡及系统定位精度会影响记录，请检查定位权限并在开阔区域重试。地图内容与可用性以 Apple 当地服务为准。

数据保存在哪里？

当前版本的核心行程、花费和轨迹数据主要保存在设备本地。你可以主动创建备份或导出报告。

如何备份、恢复或导出 PDF？

在“我的”中进入“备份恢复”，可导出或导入本地 ZIP。ZIP 包含行程资料、费用和关联费用图片，不包含完整 GPS 轨迹点。当前不提供自动 iCloud 备份。导入时可选择合并或替换；PDF 报告通过系统分享面板保存或发送。

删除 App 后还能恢复吗？

如果没有提前备份，删除 App 可能同时删除本地应用数据。卸载前请保存 ZIP、PDF 等所需文件；ZIP 无法完整恢复 GPS 轨迹点。

需要账号或付费吗？

无需注册，没有订阅或 App 内购买；现有行程、GPS 记录、费用、统计和导出功能免费使用。

支持哪些语言和货币？

支持 English 和简体中文，未支持的系统语言回退到英文。费用金额目前统一使用人民币（CNY），切换语言不会转换货币。

Frequently Asked Questions

Why is location permission required?

It is used to record GPS routes and calculate mileage. Background permission is needed if you want an active trip to continue recording while the app is backgrounded or the screen is locked.

Why does the app show “Tracking stopped” after reopening?

If the app is terminated, closed manually, or tracking is interrupted, the trip remains saved. You can recover tracking after reopening, and saved mileage is preserved.

Why might tunnel mileage be lower?

GPS may be unavailable in tunnels or underground parking. To avoid false jumps, TrackAlong may exclude distance across long GPS gaps.

Why can mileage change slightly while parked?

GPS may drift while stationary. TrackAlong filters short movements and poor locations, but small inaccuracies can still occur.

Does location tracking continue after completing a trip?

No continuous tracking should continue for the completed trip. The operating system may still show recent location use.

How do I review a recorded route?

Open a trip from Trips to review its recorded route, mileage, time, and expenses. Additional map tools are not exposed in this release.

Why can a map position look offset?

The iOS app uses Apple Maps. GPS reception, buildings, and device location accuracy can affect recorded positions. Check permissions and retry outdoors. Map content and availability depend on Apple’s local services.

Where is my data stored?

Core trip, expense, and route data is primarily stored locally on your device. You may create backups or export reports.

How do I back up, restore, or export a PDF?

Open Backup & Restore from Profile to export or import a local ZIP. Backups contain trip details, expenses, and associated expense images, but not complete GPS track points. Automatic iCloud backup is not provided. Imports can merge or replace data; PDF reports are saved or sent through the system share sheet.

Can I recover data after deleting the app?

Without a prior backup, deleting the app may remove locally stored app data. Save the ZIP and any reports you need before uninstalling. ZIP backups cannot fully restore GPS track points.

Do I need an account or payment?

No registration, subscription, or in-app purchase is required. Trip recording, expenses, statistics, and exports are free.

Which languages and currencies are supported?

English and Simplified Chinese are supported, with English fallback. Expense amounts use CNY in both languages; changing language does not convert currency.

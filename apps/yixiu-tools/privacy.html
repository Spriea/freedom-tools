了解本地处理、系统权限与数据管理。

Local processing, system permissions, and data controls.

政策范围

本政策适用于一休工具 iOS App。更新日期：2026 年 10 月 2 日。无需注册账号。App 不使用广告追踪，不向开发者服务器上传图片、音频、文档或相册分析结果，不集成第三方广告或统计 SDK。

Scope

This policy applies to the Yixiu Tools iOS app. Updated October 2, 2026. No account is required. The app does not use advertising tracking, upload your images, audio, documents, or photo-analysis results to developer servers, or include third-party advertising or analytics SDKs.

本地内容与记录

导入的图片、PDF、音视频、文本与识别结果主要在设备本地处理。设置、收藏、最近使用、使用记录及部分工具历史保存在本机；OCR 历史可在设置中关闭或清除。AI 工具使用设备上的 Apple 模型，不回退到云端。语音转写仅使用受支持的本地识别，系统可能需要联网下载语言模型。

Local content and records

Images, PDFs, audio/video, text, and recognition results are processed locally. Settings, favorites, recent usage, usage records, and selected tool histories are stored on your device. OCR history can be disabled or cleared in Settings. AI uses Apple on-device models without a cloud fallback. Speech transcription uses supported on-device recognition; the system may download language models over the network.

照片与删除

普通选图使用系统选择器。照片清理仅在你点击扫描时申请相册读写授权，支持完整和有限授权。读取可访问照片的元数据和本地资源，生成指纹、相似特征和模糊评分；可清理缓存保存在系统缓存目录。扫描不会下载 iCloud 原图，不会自动删除。删除须由你选择并确认，再由系统处理；已删除内容可能进入“最近删除”，实际释放空间与估算可能不同。有限授权下仅分析你允许访问的内容。

Photos and deletion

Ordinary imports use system pickers. Photo cleanup requests read/write access when you start a scan and supports full or limited access. It reads accessible metadata and local resources to produce fingerprints, similarity features, and blur scores. Disposable analysis caches are stored in the system cache directory. Scanning does not download iCloud originals or automatically delete photos. Deletion requires your selection and confirmation and is performed by the system. Items may enter Recently Deleted; actual reclaimed space can differ from estimates. Limited access restricts analysis to photos you allow.

系统权限

相机用于扫码、拍摄和扫描；照片添加权限用于保存结果；麦克风用于分贝等音频功能；语音识别用于本地转写；通知用于你设置的提醒。相关权限按需请求，可在 iOS 设置中管理或撤回，拒绝后对应功能可能不可用。分贝仪不保存环境录音。

Permissions

Camera access supports scanning and capture. Add-to-Photos access saves results. Microphone access supports audio features such as sound-level measurement. Speech recognition supports local transcription. Notifications support reminders you configure. Permissions are requested as needed and can be revoked in iOS Settings; affected features may become unavailable. Sound-level measurement does not save ambient recordings.

联网、购买与外部服务

App 启动时及手动检查更新时，会向 Apple 查询 App Store ID、所在地区对应的公开版本信息，不发送你的照片、文件或文本。网络服务可能处理 IP 地址等连接信息。购买、订阅与恢复购买由 Apple StoreKit 处理，开发者不获取你的支付卡信息。打开官网、隐私政策、技术支持或用户选择的链接，会离开 App 前往外部浏览器，相关网站适用其自身政策。

Network, purchases, and external services

Startup and manual update checks query Apple for public version information using the App Store ID and region; they do not send your photos, files, or text. Network services may process connection information such as IP addresses. Apple StoreKit handles purchases, subscriptions, and restoration; the developer does not receive your payment-card information. Website, policy, support, and user-selected links open an external browser, where the destination site’s policies apply.

分享、保存与数据管理

只有在你主动导出、保存或分享时，内容才会传给你选择的系统服务或接收方。iCloud 照片及设备备份由系统和你的设置管理。可在 App 设置清理相关历史，在系统中管理授权；卸载通常会移除 App 本地数据，但已导出文件、系统照片及系统备份不会因此自动删除。

Sharing, saving, and data management

Exporting, saving, or sharing sends content to the system service or recipient you choose. iCloud Photos and device backups are controlled by the system and your settings. Clear supported histories in the app and manage permissions in iOS. Uninstalling normally removes app-local data, but does not automatically remove exported files, system photos, or system backups.

技术支持与政策更新

如果你主动联系支持，我们会处理你提供的邮件地址、问题描述和必要诊断信息，用于回复和解决问题。请不要发送完整相册、身份证件、支付信息等敏感内容。政策更新会在本页公布；隐私问题请联系 somerking@foxmail.com。

Support and policy changes

When you contact support, we process the email address, issue description, and necessary diagnostic information you provide to respond and troubleshoot. Do not send entire photo libraries, identity documents, or payment details. Policy changes are posted here. For privacy questions, contact somerking@foxmail.com.
